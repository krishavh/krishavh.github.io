Privacy policy · effective 2026-09-06

NoBonk

NoBonk uses your phone's camera and an on-device vision model to warn you before you walk into a person or obstacle. All processing happens on the phone.

What we collect

Nothing. NoBonk has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests. The app does not request the Internet permission.

What stays on your device

The detection-event history and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete them at any time from inside the app, or by uninstalling it.

Optional location tag (off by default)

If you turn it on in the history screen, NoBonk stores a coarse location with each close-call event so a map of your close-call spots can be shown. It stays on the device, can be switched off again, and all stored locations can be deleted at any time. Precise location is never requested.

Camera and sensors

Camera frames and motion-sensor readings are processed in memory on the device and are never saved as images or uploaded anywhere.

Children

NoBonk was built by a student for science-fair and app-challenge use. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
