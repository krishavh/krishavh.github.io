Privacy policy · effective 2026-09-06

SafeBell

SafeBell is a data dashboard about school-zone traffic and crashes built from public datasets (Caltrans, SWITRS/TIMS, OpenStreetMap). It collects nothing from you.

What we collect

Nothing. SafeBell has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and makes no network requests. The app does not request the Internet permission.

What stays on your device

Any measurements or settings the app saves are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete them at any time from inside the app, or by uninstalling it.

Camera and sensors

Camera frames and motion-sensor readings are processed in memory on the device and are never saved as images or uploaded anywhere.

Children

SafeBell was built by a student for science-fair and app-challenge use. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
